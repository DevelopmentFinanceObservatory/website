## How to interpret

HIV aid is gross ODA disbursements under CRS purpose code 13040, STD control including HIV/AIDS, in current US dollars. HIV work recorded under other codes, such as health systems or reproductive health, is left out.

The Global Fund reports to the CRS as a provider. Money donors give the Global Fund is counted once, under the Global Fund.

People living with HIV, coverage of antiretroviral therapy, and AIDS-related deaths are UNAIDS estimates.

The table lists countries the CRS records as HIV aid recipients from {years[0]} to {LAST_YEAR} that received $1m or more in {LAST_YEAR} or have 50,000 or more people living with HIV. The dot strip holds those with 50,000 or more people living with HIV and some aid in {LAST_YEAR}.
