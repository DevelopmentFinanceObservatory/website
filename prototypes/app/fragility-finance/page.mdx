## How to interpret

The [OECD's States of Fragility 2026 update](https://www.oecd.org/en/publications/states-of-fragility-2026-update_866ee7f4-en/full-report.html) sets the {profiles.data.length} contexts, their tier, and their rank.

ODA is split into pillars by purpose code, following [States of Fragility 2025, Annex A](https://www.oecd.org/en/publications/states-of-fragility-2025_81982370-en.html): humanitarian is codes 72010, 72040, 72050, 73010, and 74020; peace is codes 15210 to 15261 and the governance codes 15110 to 15190 that the OECD counts as secondary peace; development is every other code.

ODA's share of money in is ODA over ODA, other official flows (OOF), remittances, and FDI inflows. OOF is official finance that is not ODA, mostly loans on near-market terms. A net FDI outflow counts as zero.

Debt service to 2025 is what each context paid. Later years are principal and interest scheduled on debt held at the end of 2025, before any new borrowing. For contexts in arrears or restructuring they show what is due, which can far exceed what is paid.

A group's series sums the contexts that hold all ten years, unless that leaves out 5% or more of the 2024 total; then it sums every figure held. The key shows how many contexts a figure covers where that is fewer than all.
