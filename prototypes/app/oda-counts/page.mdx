## Notes

- Grant equivalents can exceed gross disbursements (as for the United Kingdom from 2023) because private sector instruments and debt relief are counted on a different basis. Net disbursements can also sit above gross (as for Finland in 2020).
- The note on non-grant instruments in How the steps are computed uses gross non-grant disbursements, published from 2018 to 2024, and appears only when the gap does not exceed them.
- Constant prices are in 2024 US dollars, converted with OECD DAC deflators from ONE's conversion tables to remove donor inflation and exchange-rate movements against the dollar.
- 2025 figures are preliminary. Most donors lack 2025 commitments and breakdowns until the OECD publishes final figures.
- Group totals, such as DAC countries, omit any year where fewer than half of the usual reporting countries reported a series. Group totals also omit grant equivalents before 2018, which only some members reported, and 2025 commitments, breakdowns, and in-donor refugee costs, which only two countries reported.
